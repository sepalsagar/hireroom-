## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a warm, welcoming, consumer-centric ethos tailored for high-trust discovery and booking experiences. The interface projects unpretentious hospitality, visual calm, and clarity, stripping away corporate detachment in favor of immediate legibility and tactile delight.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Approachable Minimalism with Warmth:** Built upon crisp, luminous canvases anchored by soft, warm neutral backdrops, eliminating visual noise so high-quality photography and content shine.
- **Friendly Geometry:** A cohesive blend of gentle card roundings, circular focal interactions, and iconic pill-shaped search modules that invite physical engagement.
- **Human-Centric Directness:** Microcopy and component layouts prioritize simplicity and warmth over robotic systemic jargon.
- **Delicate Depth:** Surfaces rely on layered tonal fills, ultra-crisp hairline dividers, and whisper-soft diffused shadows rather than harsh physical extrusions.

## Colors

The palette balances a clean, light-filled foundation with an iconic, energetic coral-rose signature accent.

- **Primary (`#FF385C`):** The signature vibrant coral tone, reserved for primary calls to action, high-priority highlights, and active navigation states.
- **Secondary (`#E00B41`):** A deeper, richer crimson-rose used for pressed states, destructive accent nuances, and high-contrast badges.
- **Tertiary (`#008489`):** A classic balanced teal, deployed sparingly for verified credentials, host trust markers, and secondary informational highlights.
- **Neutral Core (`#222222`):** A soft, deep charcoal ink that replaces harsh absolute black, providing superior reading comfort across long browsing sessions.
- **Neutral Tonal Foundations:**
  - Surface Pure: `#FFFFFF`
  - Surface Subdued (Canvas / Neutral Light): `#F7F7F7`
  - Surface Muted (Borders / Chips / Hover Wells): `#F2F2F2`
  - Outline & Hairlines: `#DDDDDD`
  - Secondary Ink (Subtitles & Captions): `#717171`

## Typography

Typography relies on clean, geometric clarity with neutral proportions and humanist warmth. The typographic hierarchy uses modest weights (Regular 400, Medium 500, Semi-Bold 600) rather than heavy black styles, sustaining a breathable, sophisticated editorial air.

- **Primary Headings (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`):** Executed in 600 weight with tight, negative letter tracking to maintain punch and compactness across titles and destination labels.
- **Section & Card Titles (`headline-md`, `headline-sm`):** Ground content groups with immediate scannability.
- **Body Text:** Neutral, open apertures optimized for dense metadata, property rules, and conversational review bodies.
- **Action & Metadata Labels:** Weighted to 500 or 600 to ensure clear interactive affordance on buttons, tab items, rating tags, and price summaries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around edge padding and natural rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1128px+ max content container):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` outer margin and `1.5rem` gutters. Listing cards flow naturally across 4, 3, or 2-column configurations.
- **Tablet (744px - 1127px):** 8 columns with `2rem` outer margin and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<744px):** 4 columns with `1.5rem` outer margin and `1rem` gutters, prioritizing full-bleed or peek-carousel media wrappers.

### Vertical Rhythm & Density
- Component internal spacing stays airy. Compact listings maintain `0.75rem` between imagery and caption blocks.
- Page sections transition with generous `space-xl` gaps to maintain visual decompression during deep browsing.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy, dramatic drop shadows in favor of ambient lift, delicate boundaries, and tonal resting planes.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Baseline `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F7F7` tonal foundation with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Lift / Resting Cards):** Surrounded by a crisp `#DDDDDD` hairline border (1px), augmented on hover by an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 6px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Search Bar & Overlays):** Interactive floating components (such as pill search modules and map toggle pills) utilize a balanced drop shadow: `0 3px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sheet Menus):** High-priority focus layers cast a broad, low-saturation shadow: `0 16px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12), 0 4px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a tiered corner-radius hierarchy that reinforces hospitality and tactile approachability:

- **Base Radius (`rounded`, 8px):** Standard interactive surfaces including primary buttons, input text boxes, review summary boxes, and dropdown menus.
- **Card Radius (`rounded-lg`, 14px / 16px):** Dedicated to content containers, listing photo carousels, and gallery previews, delivering soft contours without feeling childish.
- **Pill Radius (Full / 9999px):** Applied to floating search strips, tag filters, category selectors, and primary floating action buttons.
- **Circular (50%):** Dedicated to user avatars, directional pagination buttons, and heart/favorite action orbs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#FF385C` with white `#FFFFFF` text, `label-md` weight, 8px corner radius, and subtle hover deepening to `#E00B41`. Padding: 12px vertical by 24px horizontal.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, `#222222` 1px border, `#222222` text, with a `#F7F7F7` fill shift on hover.
- **Ghost:** Borderless, `#222222` text, with a `#F7F7F7` rounded background on interaction.
