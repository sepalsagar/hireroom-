Honest Answers

Frequently asked hiring questions

No jargon, no evasive marketing. Here is how we safeguard both candidate well-being and hiring signal.

How does HireRoom actively decrease candidate anxiety?

expand_more
Every candidate receives their own private Practice Lounge 48 hours before the interview. They can run their webcam check, experiment with the exact IDE and keyboard shortcuts, and review an outline of the round's structure so there are no ambush surprises.

Do we need to install any heavy browser extensions or plugins?

expand_more
Zero installs or extensions required. HireRoom runs directly in any modern browser via pure WebRTC and WebAssembly. Candidates can join effortlessly on their personal machine without security permission friction.

Does HireRoom integrate with our ATS like Greenhouse or Ashby?

expand_more
Yes. We offer deep bi-directional synchronization with Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, and Workday. Interview links are auto-generated when scheduling, and rubrics plus session narratives sync directly back to candidate profiles upon round completion.

How does the "Mutual Conversation Pace Detection" feature work?

expand_more
Our silent coach tracks speaking ratio and pauses. If an interviewer is unintentionally monopolizing the dialogue or interrupting too quickly, a gentle private reminder nudges them to create space for the candidate to synthesize thoughts.
